About

Derive it. Build it. Measure it. Find out why it's wrong.

I like knowing why a circuit works, not just that it does. I'd rather derive the equations than copy a reference design. Then I build it, put it on the scope, and chase every place the hardware disagrees with the math. That gap is usually where I learn the most.

Before engineering, I spent 12 years in the Air Force and Air National Guard as an F-16 crew chief and NDT inspector, finding cracks nobody else could see on aircraft in Arizona, South Korea, Japan, California and Pennsylvania. The habits stuck: don't accept the first answer, trust the measurement over the assumption, and write the procedure so someone else can repeat it.

Right now I'm going deep on analog design and power systems. I do my best work when I'm handed an unsolved problem, a bench, and room to figure it out.
